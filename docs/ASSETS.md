# Preview assets

## Landing campaign

The current full-width hero uses `public/images/campaign/every-step-v3.webp`; the preloved section uses `fresh-chapters-v3.webp`. Both are new original, unbranded AI-generated photographs made with the built-in tool. Their PNG originals are retained, and their complete prompts/paths are recorded in `CAMPAIGN_PROMPTS_V3.json`. They use soft cream/sage lighting and shoe subjects. They are concept imagery and do not establish the authenticity or availability of inventory.

`public/images/campaign/next-move-v2.png` and `collection-worlds-v2.png` are original AI-generated editorial footwear concept photographs created with the built-in image-generation tool. Their WebP siblings are the browser assets; the original PNGs are retained. The collection sheet has three equal photographic columns for Men, Women and Kids, cropped by CSS without image stretching. No competitor photograph or logo was copied. These concepts do not depict genuine branded stock offered for sale. See `LANDING_UPGRADE.md` and `CAMPAIGN_PROMPTS.json` for saved paths and complete prompts.

`public/images/catalog/orbit.png` is an earlier original AI-generated concept sneaker render with a baked dark background. It is retained as a source asset; its dark homepage presentation was removed in the current redesign.

The remaining footwear and lifestyle preview photographs were downloaded from Unsplash image URLs and are bundled locally. They demonstrate the catalog layout, with visible sample labels. Replace them with photographs of your actual products before opening sales. Preview product records use the brand visible in their reference photograph and do not assert authenticity, availability or ownership.

## Sixty original demo pairs

`public/images/demo/men-atlas.png`, `women-atlas.png` and `kids-atlas.png` are original AI-generated studio catalogs made for this upgrade. Each sheet contains 20 distinct shoe designs in a five-column, four-row layout. `ShoePhoto` displays one individual photo through CSS positioning in cards, galleries, saved pairs, bags and admin previews; it never shows the full sheet as a product. The images are bundled locally and never require third-party hotlinks.

Each demo record has an original name, individual description, colour/material notes and a distinct PKR price. Popular brand labels demonstrate category selection; the unbranded concepts are not representations of genuine branded stock or items offered for sale. No demo records are inserted into the live database.
